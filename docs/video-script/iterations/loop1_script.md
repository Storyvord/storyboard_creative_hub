# Creative Hub — Demo Video Script (V1)

**Through-line:** A screenplay walks in. A shot-ready film walks out — scenes, cast, locations, and a storyboard that looks like the same movie from page one to the last frame. One workspace, one continuous thread, and a single rule the product never breaks: the face you cast in scene one is the face that shows up in scene forty.

---

## Chapter 1 — Script
**Runtime target:** ~75s  **Word count:** 175
**Visual opening:** A PDF icon floating over the dashed-border upload zone. The cursor drags it in.
**Artifact carried in from previous chapter:** The screenplay PDF.
**Artifact handed to next chapter:** The locked, parsed screenplay as a real US-letter page.

### Narration
> Every film starts the same way. Someone hands you a PDF.
>
> [VISUAL: PDF drops into the upload zone. Emerald ring pulses around the loader. Small chyron: "Converting screenplay · 30–90 seconds."]
>
> Final Draft, Word, even an RTF someone scanned out of a typewriter — it doesn't matter. Drop it in.
>
> [VISUAL: Loader resolves. The screen rebuilds the script as a real page — drop-shadowed, 720 pixels wide, slug lines in bold caps, dialogue centered, transitions hard right. It looks like a screenplay because it is one.]
>
> What you get back isn't a copy. It's the same script, parsed into scenes, characters, dialogue — every line typed to the right element.
>
> [VISUAL: Cursor rests on a slug line. Tab key overlays — `⌘1` jumps to scene heading, `⌘2` to action, `⌘3` to character. Hold for one beat.]
>

>
> [VISUAL: Amber pulsing banner — "Review parsed script." The cursor hits Confirm. Banner clears.]
>
> Lock the script.
>
> That's the moment the rest of the film becomes real.

### Director notes
- Capture the conversion loader's emerald ring and the small pulsing dot — that's the product's visual signature.
- When the page rebuilds, hold for two full beats. The "screenplay-as-document" framing is the strongest visual identity in the entire product.
- Show the keyboard shortcut overlays Linear-style. Small mono pill, lower-third. `⌘1–7` is the flex.
- Do not show the analytics modal in V1 — it's a distraction from the through-line. Save it for a deep-dive video.
- The word "Lock" is the artifact handoff. The amber banner clearing into a clean page should be the visual punctuation under that single word.
